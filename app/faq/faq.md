# Fridgly - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Pricing & Subscription

### Is Fridgly free to use?
Yes! Fridgly is completely free to use. All features including ** Tailored *** recipe suggestions, smart shopping lists, pantry tracking, and macro logging are available at no cost.

### Are there any usage limits?
Each account has a monthly limit of 200 AI-powered actions (API calls). This includes recipe generation, shopping list creation, cooking step generation, receipt scanning, and macro estimation. The limit resets at the beginning of each month.

### What counts as an API call?
The following actions count toward your monthly limit:
- Generating personalized recipes
- Creating a shopping list from your meal plan
- Getting AI-generated cooking steps
- Scanning a receipt to add pantry items
- Importing a recipe from a screenshot
- Estimating macros from a meal photo
- Generating recipes based on your macros goals

### Will there be a premium version?
We're focused on providing a great free experience for now. If we introduce premium features in the future, we'll make sure the core functionality remains free.

## Shopping List

### Where does my shopping list come from?
Your shopping list is automatically generated from your weekly meal plan. When you add recipes to your daily plans, Fridgly intelligently combines all the ingredients you'll need for the week.

### How does the smart shopping list work?
Fridgly's shopping list is smart in several ways:
- **Combines ingredients**: If multiple recipes need onions, they're combined into one entry
- **Converts units**: Different measurements are converted and combined (e.g., cups + tablespoons)
- **Excludes pantry items**: Ingredients already in your pantry are automatically excluded
- **Excludes staples**: Common kitchen staples (salt, oil, pepper) are not added

### Can I manually add items to my shopping list?
Yes! You can tap the "+" button to manually add any item to your shopping list, even if it's not from a recipe.

### What happens when I check off an item?
When you mark an item as purchased (checked), it's automatically added to your pantry. This keeps your pantry inventory up to date as you shop.

### Why are some ingredients missing from my shopping list?
Items are excluded if:
- They're already in your pantry
- They're marked as a staple item (like salt, oil, or flour)
- You've already purchased them this week

## Pantry

### How do I add items to my pantry?
There are several ways to add items to your pantry:
1. **Check off grocery items**: When you mark shopping list items as purchased
2. **Scan a receipt**: Use the receipt scanner to automatically extract and add items
3. **Manual entry**: Tap the "+" button to add items manually
4. **Staple items**: Add common kitchen staples that you always have on hand

### What are staple items?
Staple items are common ingredients you always keep stocked (like salt, cooking oil, flour, sugar). These are excluded from your shopping list since you typically don't need to buy them.

### How does the "consumed" feature work?
When you tap the "comsumed" button on a pantry item, it reduces the remaining quantity by 25%. This helps you track how much of each item you have left without needing exact measurements.

### Does my pantry affect recipe suggestions?
Yes! When you use "Generate from Pantry," Fridgly creates recipes based on what you already have. It shows:
- Full matches: Recipes you can make with current ingredients
- Partial matches: Recipes that need just 1-2 more items

## Recipes & Meal Planning

### How are recipes personalized for me?
Fridgly learns your preferences through:
- **Dietary restrictions**: Halal, Kosher, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.
- **Allergies**: Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut-free, etc.
- **Cuisine preferences**: Italian, Mexican, Asian, Mediterranean, etc.
- **Macro preferences**: High protein, low carb, calorie-conscious, etc.
- **Swipe feedback**: Swiping left/right helps the AI learn your taste

### What do the swipes do?
- **Swipe right**: Adds the recipe to your cookbook and tells the AI you like this style
- **Swipe left**: Skips the recipe and helps the AI avoid similar recipes in the future

### Can I import my own recipes?
Yes! You can:
- **Screenshot import**: Take a photo of a recipe (from a book, website, etc.) and Fridgly will extract the details
- **Manual entry**: Add recipes manually with your own ingredients and instructions

### How do I plan my week?
1. Browse or generate recipes
2. Add recipes to specific days
3. Your shopping list automatically updates
4. View your week in the calendar view

## Cooking Mode

### What is Cooking Mode?
Cooking Mode provides step-by-step guidance while you cook. Each step includes:
- Clear instructions
- Estimated time for each step
- Built-in timers
- Ingredient prep reminders

### Can I customize the cooking instructions?
Yes! You can adjust:
- **Skill level** (1-5): Simpler instructions for beginners, more concise for experts
- **Detail level** (1-5): How much explanation each step includes
- **Serving size** (1-8): Instructions scale to your portion needs

### Why are cooking steps generated separately?
To save your monthly API calls, cooking steps are only generated when you actually start cooking a recipe, not when browsing. This means you can browse unlimited recipes without using your quota.

## Macros & Nutrition

### How do I track my macros?
1. Go to the Macros tab
2. Log meals by type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack)
3. Either enter manually or take a photo for AI estimation
4. View your daily and weekly progress

### How accurate is the photo macro estimation?
The AI provides reasonable estimates based on visual analysis. For precise tracking, we recommend:
- Using it for general guidance
- Manually adjusting values when you know exact portions
- Logging recipe-based meals for better accuracy

### Can I set custom macro goals?
Yes! You can set your daily targets for:
- Calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fats

### What is "Generate from Macros"?
This feature creates recipes specifically designed to help you hit your remaining macro targets for the day. Perfect for meal prepping or when you need a meal with specific nutritional content.

## Receipt Scanning

### How do I scan a receipt?
1. Go to Pantry
2. Tap "Scan Receipt"
3. Take a photo of your grocery receipt
4. Review the extracted items
5. Confirm to add them to your pantry

### What receipts work best?
For best results:
- Use clear, well-lit photos
- Ensure text is readable
- Standard grocery store receipts work best
- Handwritten lists may not work as well

## Troubleshooting

### Why am I seeing "API limit reached"?
You've used your 200 monthly AI actions. Your limit resets on the 1st of each month. In the meantime, you can:
- Browse your saved cookbook recipes
- Manually add items to pantry and shopping list
- Use the cooking mode for already-generated recipes

### Recipes aren't matching my dietary restrictions
Make sure your preferences are up to date:
1. Go to Profile > Preferences
2. Review your dietary restrictions and allergies
3. Save any changes
4. Generate new recipes

### The app isn't syncing properly
Try these steps:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh on any screen
3. Log out and log back in
4. If issues persist, contact support

### My shopping list seems wrong
This usually happens when:
- Pantry items are out of date (check and update your pantry)
- Recipes were removed after the list was generated (regenerate the list)
- Ingredients were manually removed from the list

## Tips & Best Practices

### Getting the best recipe recommendations
1. Complete the onboarding preferences thoroughly
2. Swipe on recipes regularly to train the AI
3. Update your preferences as your tastes change
4. Use "Generate from Pantry" to reduce food waste

### Saving API calls
- Browse your cookbook instead of generating new recipes daily
- Plan your whole week at once instead of day-by-day
- Use manual entry for simple items
- Generate cooking steps only when you're ready to cook

### Keeping your pantry accurate
- Scan receipts right after shopping
- Use the "eaten" button as you use items
- Review and clean up your pantry weekly
- Mark staple items so they're excluded from shopping lists
